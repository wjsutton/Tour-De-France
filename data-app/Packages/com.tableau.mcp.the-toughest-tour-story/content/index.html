2 · Five ways to suffer

Abandons

So was it tougher back then?

On abandons, yes: about 69% of riders quit each Tour before the First World War, about 21% since 2000.

Five ways to suffer

But quitting is only one way to suffer.

A Tour is also hard when it is long, high, close or fast. Each gets a wedge of the wheel.

Distance

1926, the longest.

Lucien Buysse's Tour covered 5,745 km, almost double a modern route.

Elevation

Up to 2,802 m.

The Cime de la Bonette: first in 1962, then again in 1964, 1993, 2008 and 2024.

Competition

Won on the final day.

Jean Robic in 1947 and Jan Janssen in 1968 only took the lead on the last stage.

Competition

The Merckx years.

Merckx led most stages in 1969–72 and 1974, so his bars almost vanish. They return in 1975, when Thévenet beat him.

Pace

Faster, not easier.

Winners averaged about 25 kph in the 1900s. Armstrong's 2005 record was voided; 2026 averaged 43.3 kph, the fastest Tour ever.

3 · Read a wheel

The wheel

Put the five together.

Each Tour becomes a wheel; a wedge that reaches the tyre is the record. In 1919 the pink wedge is full: 86% quit.

1919 · 2026

Now compare 2026.

Far fewer riders quit and the route is shorter. But look at the green wedge: speed.

4 · Classement

Classement

1919 was the toughest Tour.

Average the five wedges: 1919 scores 87, first of 113. 1922 (81) and 1949 (80) come next.

Classement

And the easiest? The very first.

1903: six stages, low roads, and Maurice Garin led from start to finish. It scores 44.

5 · Shape shift

Shape shift

The Tour never got easier.

Average each era and the score barely moves: 67, 68, 64. The pain moved round the wheel, from abandons (72) before the war to pace (93) since 1990.

Methods

Each factor is scaled 0–100 against its record, so the toughest Tour on that measure scores 100. Competition is the share of stages the eventual winner did not lead. A Tour's toughness score is the plain average of its five scaled factors, weighted equally. The era wheels average every Tour in 1903–1939, 1947–1989 and 1990 onwards.
